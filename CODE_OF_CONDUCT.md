# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Project participants are expected to make collaboration respectful, precise, and welcoming. Technical disagreement is useful when it addresses evidence and behavior without diminishing the people involved.

## Expected conduct

Communicate constructively, assume good intent while asking for clarification, respect different backgrounds and levels of experience, and keep feedback focused on the work. Protect private information and never post another person’s manuscript, credentials, or identifying data without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact information on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or take other proportionate action when this policy is violated.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, review threads, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
